Use the dashboard and notifications page to monitor confirmed, pending, or conflict reservation states.

Frequently asked questions

  • Bookings are shown with room number, date, time, and reservation status.
  • Pending requests may need staff review before confirmation.
  • Conflicts appear when a room overlaps with another reservation.
  • Reservation changes are handled in the booking management section.
